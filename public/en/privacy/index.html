2. This website

This website uses no cookies, no tracking, no analytics and no external fonts or scripts. To deliver the pages, the web server briefly processes your IP address and your browser's request; it does not keep access logs. The site runs on an Oracle Cloud server in Frankfurt (Germany). HTTPS certificates come from Let's Encrypt; no visitor data is passed on for this.

3. Light/dark setting

If you choose light or dark with the switch, your browser stores this choice locally (localStorage). It never leaves your device and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

4. Links

Links to other sites (GitHub, Ko-fi, yatb.gladi.de) are normal links; the privacy policies of those sites apply there. The bots have their own privacy information, e.g. yatb.gladi.de/en/privacy/.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Contact the operator (section 1) to exercise your rights.
